About Tone Sense

An AI powered speech emotion recognition application

Tone Sense is a web application that analyses the emotional tone behind your voice. Record a short voice note and our AI model will detect your emotional state and provide you with instant, personalised feedback.

How it works

When you record your voice, the audio is processed in real time. A mel spectrogram is extracted from your recording along with delta features that capture how your voice changes over time. These are passed through a deep learning model trained on the RAVDESS emotional speech dataset, which classifies your voice into one of seven emotions — Calm, Happy, Sad, Angry, Fearful, Disgust, or Surprised.

Tone Sense does not store your voice recordings. Once your audio has been analysed it is immediately deleted from the server. Only the the detected emotion and confidence score are saved to your personal history. Your email address is encrypted and never stored in plain text. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your dashboard.
