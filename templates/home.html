Speech Analysis

Happy

High pitch & fast tempo indicate excitement and engagement.

Sad

Softer, lower voice with slower speech and falling intonation.

Angry

Louder, sharper tone with faster, forceful delivery and tense pitch shifts.

Surprised

Sudden jump in pitch and energy with a quick, sharp vocal onset.

Neutral

Balanced pitch, steady rhythm, and minimal expressive variation.

Calm

Even, steady voice with relaxed pacing and smooth tone.

Fearful

Higher, slightly shaky or breathy voice with rushed or uneven pacing.

Disgust

Tight, slightly lowered tone with clipped or strained delivery.
